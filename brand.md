## Typography — DM Sans + DM Mono

- DM Sans for interface copy and headings.
- DM Mono for amounts, wallet addresses, and transaction identifiers.

## Gradients

- `bg-brand-bg`: subtle background depth for a page header or large empty area.
- `bg-brand-accent`: a warm accent for non-critical feature treatments.

Do not use the accent gradient beneath money amounts, form fields, or body copy.

## Voice

Be human, clear, and reassuring. Say what will happen before a payment is signed. Avoid hype, corporate language, and manufactured urgency.
